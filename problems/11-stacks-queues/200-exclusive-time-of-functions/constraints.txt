1 <= n <= 100
2 <= m <= 500
m is an even number.
0 <= function_id < n
0 <= timestamp <= 10^9
Logs are sorted chronologically by timestamp. Logs are valid (every start has a matching end, etc.).
Calls are properly nested: every end log closes the most recently started call that is still running.
A log that comes right after an end log at time t has timestamp at least t + 1.
