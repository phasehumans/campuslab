You are given the root of a binary search tree (BST), where the values of exactly two nodes of the tree were swapped by mistake. Recover the tree without changing its structure.

Print the level-order BFS traversal of the recovered BST (omitting trailing nulls).

**Input Format:**
- A single line of BFS representation of the broken BST.

**Output Format:**
- The BFS representation of the recovered BST.
