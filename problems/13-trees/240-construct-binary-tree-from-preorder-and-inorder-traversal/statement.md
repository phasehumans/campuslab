Given two integer arrays preorder and inorder where preorder is the preorder traversal of a binary tree and inorder is the inorder traversal of the same tree, construct and return the binary tree (printed as its level-order BFS serialization, omitting trailing nulls).

**Input Format:**
- First line: integer n (number of nodes)
- Second line: n space-separated integers representing preorder
- Third line: n space-separated integers representing inorder

**Output Format:**
- The BFS representation of the constructed tree.
