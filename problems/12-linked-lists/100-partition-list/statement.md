Given the head of a linked list and a value `x`, partition it such that all nodes less than `x` come before nodes greater than or equal to `x`.

You must preserve the original relative order of the nodes in each of the two partitions.

**Input Format:**
- First line: two integers `n` (number of elements) and `x` (partition value)
- Second line: `n` space-separated integers

**Output Format:**
- Space-separated integers representing the partitioned list
