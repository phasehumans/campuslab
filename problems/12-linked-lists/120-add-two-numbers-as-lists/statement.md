You are given two non-empty linked lists representing two non-negative integers. The digits are stored in reverse order, and each of their nodes contains a single digit. Add the two numbers and return the sum as a linked list in reverse order.

The numbers have no leading zeros, except the number 0 itself (a single node with value 0).

**Input Format:**
- First line: two integers `n1` and `n2` (lengths of the two lists)
- Second line: `n1` space-separated integers representing digits of list 1 (reversed)
- Third line: `n2` space-separated integers representing digits of list 2 (reversed)

**Output Format:**
- Space-separated integers representing the sum list digits in reverse order
