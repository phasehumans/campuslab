Given a 32-bit unsigned integer, reverse its bits and print the resulting number.

For example, the 32-bit representation of 13 is 00000000000000000000000000001101. Reversing it gives 10110000000000000000000000000000, which is 2952790016.

**Input Format:**
- A single line containing a non-negative integer n (fits in 32-bit unsigned)

**Output Format:**
- The integer obtained after reversing all 32 bits of n
