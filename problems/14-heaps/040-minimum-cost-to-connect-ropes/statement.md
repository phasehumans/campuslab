There are `n` ropes of different lengths. You need to connect these ropes into a single rope. The cost to connect two ropes of lengths A and B is A + B. You want to connect all ropes with the minimum total cost.

**Input Format:**
- First line: an integer `n` (number of ropes).
- Second line: `n` space-separated integers representing the rope lengths.

**Output Format:**
- A single integer representing the minimum cost to connect all ropes. If `n <= 1`, the cost is `0`.
