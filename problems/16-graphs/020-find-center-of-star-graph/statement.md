A star graph is a graph of N nodes (labeled 1 to N) with exactly N-1 edges where one center node is connected to all other nodes. Given the N-1 edges, find the center node.

**Input Format:**
- First line: N
- Next N-1 lines: u v (undirected edge between u and v)

**Output Format:**
- An integer representing the center node of the star graph.
