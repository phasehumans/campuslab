Given a connected undirected graph with N vertices and M edges, perform a deep copy (clone) of the graph. Reconstruct the cloned graph, and print the adjacency list of the cloned graph. For each vertex from 0 to N-1, print its neighbors in sorted ascending order of their IDs.

**Input Format:**
- First line: N M
- Next M lines: u v (undirected edge between u and v)

**Output Format:**
- N lines, where each line i contains 'i: neighbor1 neighbor2 ...'
