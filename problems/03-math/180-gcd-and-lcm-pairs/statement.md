Given two integers G and L, find the number of ordered pairs (A, B) of positive integers such that their Greatest Common Divisor is G and their Least Common Multiple is L. That is, gcd(A, B) = G and lcm(A, B) = L.

If no such pairs exist, print 0.

**Input Format:**
- A single line containing two space-separated integers G and L.

**Output Format:**
- A single line containing the number of ordered pairs (A, B) that satisfy the conditions.
