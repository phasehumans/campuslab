Given an array of strings `strs`, group the anagrams together.

To make the output deterministic and testable, you must:
1. Sort the words within each group lexicographically.
2. Sort the groups lexicographically based on their first words.
3. Print each group on a new line with space-separated words.

**Input Format:**
- First line: integer `n` (number of strings)
- Second line: `n` space-separated strings of lowercase English letters.

**Output Format:**
- Each group of anagrams on a new line, space-separated, formatted according to the rules.
