Given an m x n board of characters and a string word, check if the word exists in the grid. The word can be formed from letters of horizontally or vertically adjacent cells. The same cell may not be used more than once in a single word path.

**Input Format:**
- First line: two integers m and n
- Next m lines: n space-separated characters representing the board
- Last line: the string word

**Output Format:**
- The string "true" if the word exists in the board, otherwise "false"
