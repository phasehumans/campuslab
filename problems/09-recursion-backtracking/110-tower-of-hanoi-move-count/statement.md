The Tower of Hanoi is a mathematical puzzle with three rods and n disks. The objective is to move the entire stack to another rod, obeying the rules:
1. Only one disk can be moved at a time.
2. Each move consists of taking the upper disk from one stack and placing it on another stack.
3. No larger disk may be placed on top of a smaller disk.

Given the number of disks n, calculate the minimum number of moves required to solve the puzzle recursively.

**Input Format:**
- A single integer n

**Output Format:**
- A single integer representing the minimum number of moves
