Given two strings `s` and `t`, determine whether `s` is a subsequence of `t`.

A subsequence of a string is obtained by deleting some (possibly none) of its characters without changing the relative order of the remaining characters. For example, "ace" is a subsequence of "abcde", while "aec" is not.

**Input Format:**
- First line: string `s`
- Second line: string `t`

**Output Format:**
- Print "true" if `s` is a subsequence of `t`, otherwise print "false".
